# Few-Shot Prompt Templates

## Purpose
Example Q&A pairs demonstrating expected response quality and format for the STIHL Sales Agent.

---

## Template: Product Rankings

```text
EXAMPLE 1 - Top Products Query:
User: What are our best-selling products?
Assistant: Based on the latest sales data, here are your top performers:

1. **MS 271 Farm Boss Chainsaw** - $892,450 revenue (2,847 units)
   - Growth: +15% YoY
   - Category leader

2. **BR 700 Backpack Blower** - $654,320 revenue (1,923 units)
   - Growth: +8% YoY
   - Professional segment leader

3. **FS 91 R Trimmer** - $523,180 revenue (3,412 units)
   - Growth: +22% YoY
   - Fastest growing

Key Insight: Chainsaws lead revenue, but trimmers show strongest growth trajectory.
```

## Template: Regional Analysis

```text
EXAMPLE - Regional Comparison:
User: How do our regions compare?
Assistant: Regional Performance (Last Quarter):

**Rankings by Revenue:**
1. 🥇 Southeast: $4.2M (28% of total, +12% YoY)
2. 🥈 Midwest: $3.8M (25%, +8% YoY)
3. 🥉 West: $3.1M (21%, +15% YoY)

**Key Findings:**
- West shows strongest growth momentum
- Southeast maintains volume leadership
- Northeast underperforming (-3% YoY)

**Recommendation:** Investigate Northeast decline; consider reallocating marketing spend to high-growth West region.
```

## Template: Trend Analysis

```text
EXAMPLE - Trend Query:
User: Are chainsaw sales trending up or down?
Assistant: Chainsaw sales are **trending upward** with strong momentum:

**6-Month Trend:**
| Month | Revenue | MoM Change |
|-------|---------|------------|
| Nov   | $1.24M  | +8%        |
| Oct   | $1.15M  | +5%        |
| Sep   | $1.09M  | +12%       |

**Summary:**
- Average monthly growth: +8.3%
- YoY comparison: +18%
- Key driver: MS 271 Farm Boss (34% of category)

**Implication:** Strong demand supports Q1 inventory investment.
```

---

## Usage Notes

1. Examples should match query complexity
2. Always include specific numbers
3. End with actionable insight when appropriate
4. Keep formatting consistent with example style
